Click on a card — 5 UX explorations

Five distinct answers to what happens when you tap a piece in the gallery, all built on the deployed site's design language — Manrope, IBM Plex Mono meta, the achromatic token set (#111 / #8A8A8A / #E6E6E6 / #F2F2F2 / #EEEEEE), a single blue accent #2F6BFF, r12 cards and r999 pills. The Paper "click on card" frame set the proportions and feel (split stage + ~510px info panel); V1 reproduces it, V2–V5 explore other registers. Every stage is live — click any tile to open, use close / arrows to navigate.
