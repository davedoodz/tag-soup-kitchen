Hub Directions Design system

Five masonry treatments, all carrying the same nine real records and their production media, so the layout is the only thing under review.

Titles, subtitles, roles, years, topics, and hero images were pulled from the live deployment at davidlafond.xyz — build e0764e4, 2026-08-16 — and optimized into public/images. No placeholder copy and no placeholder tiles appear on these pages.

1Variants

  • 01 Column masonry Three CSS columns, natural aspect ratios, captions on the page. The ragged column bottom is the layout — nothing is cropped and nothing is padded to match. 4211
  • 02 Justified rows Every row is flush left and right: widths are proportional to each image ratio, so the row height falls out of the content instead of a fixed grid. 4212
  • 03 Span mosaic A six-column grid on a 96px row module. Entries claim two or four columns and three to five rows, and images crop to the claim. 4213
  • 04 Lead and columns One full-measure lead at 24px, then the remaining eight records in a tighter three-column masonry with serial numbering. 4214
  • 05 Spec rails Two wide columns. Each record carries a banded spec rail — role, years, topics — so the index reads as a datasheet under the image. 4215
