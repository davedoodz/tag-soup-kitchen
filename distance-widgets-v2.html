A — Stub

284 × 134 · flips to its own back

B — Arc

284 × 134 · dark and light dressing

Real great-circle sampling, not a decorative curve: the line is 64 points of spherical interpolation flattened to the card, so arc height is the trip. It draws itself in on reveal, a live-green dot walks it, drive mode flattens to a dashed road, and same city collapses to one ring.

C — Globe

Orthographic sphere on the map widget's #e7ecf1 ground, with the map's glass label chip and .map-pin colours — accent blue for the visitor, ink for me. It librates ±16° around the route's mid-meridian so both pins stay on the near face; the far half of the great circle is drawn faint behind the sphere.
