Five ways to move a card

Same three cards in every row — only the motion changes, so you can feel each one head-to-head. Hover to preview; on 03 click to feel the press. Every avenue respects prefers-reduced-motion. Tell me the number you like and I'll wire it into the live site's widgets and project grid.

01

Spring lift

Rises and scales with a real spring — a small overshoot, then settles. linear() easing on translate + scale. Playful, tactile, still quick.

hover → springs up and settles

02

Pointer tilt

The card tilts in 3D toward your cursor with a specular sheen and a floating label (rotateX/Y + translateZ). Premium, physical, product-shot feel.

move the cursor across a card → it tilts and catches light

03

Press

Raises on hover, then physically depresses on click (scale: 0.96, shadow collapses) before springing back. Reads unmistakably as a button — best for the clickable project cards.

hover to raise · click to feel it depress

04

Reveal

Media zooms slightly inside the frame while a scrim and an "Open ↗" affordance slide up and the title lifts. Editorial; tells you there's something behind the card.

hover → media zooms, "Open" slides in

05

Glow trace

A conic-gradient border sweeps around the card on hover with a soft accent glow (@property --angle animation). Eye-catching; nice for a "featured" card, use sparingly.

hover → the accent border traces the edge

Recommendation: 01 Spring lift for the hero widgets (calm, consistent) and 03 Press for the clickable project cards (clear button feedback). 02 Tilt is the boldest single-card moment if you want one signature interaction. Say the number and I'll ship it.
