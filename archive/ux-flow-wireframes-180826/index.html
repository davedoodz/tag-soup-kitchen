David Lafond Designs · UX study

Four ways through the work.

A low-fidelity map of how visitors discover, understand, and act on the portfolio.

Frame viewport
01 Browse → filter → project → next project 4 frames · discovery loop
Key decision

Optimise for uninterrupted comparison by making project pages a lateral loop. The risk is disorientation if the next-project cue obscures a clear route back to the filtered index.

02 First visit → scan → contact 5 frames · comprehension to action
Key decision

Optimise for confidence before conversion: visitors understand the practice, see proof, then choose contact or subscribe. The risk is that two calls to action split intent at the final step.

03 Case study read 5 frames · project anatomy
Key decision

Optimise for a narrative that moves from evidence to reasoning to impact, with each section earning the next. The risk is a formulaic rhythm across projects unless media scale and section depth adapt to the story.

04 Return visitor → what’s new 4 frames · rapid re-entry
Key decision

Optimise for fast recognition by front-loading recent work and keeping “Latest” explicit. The risk is over-personalising without reliable visit history, so the interface must still make sense as a generic recency view.
